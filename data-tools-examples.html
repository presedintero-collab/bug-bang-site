How to try the examples

Download the sample files, then open the matching tool. For DataFresh, choose the BEFORE CSV, keep the three cleanup options enabled and click Clean. For DataDiff, upload both CSV files, select id in the matching-key dropdown and click Compare.

The tools run in your browser. The external Excel parsing library is loaded via CDN; avoid sensitive data without reviewing your browser environment. These examples are illustrative, not customer records, and the tools are beta software.
